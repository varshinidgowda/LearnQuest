# 🎮 LearnQuest – Turn Your Notes Into An Adventure

> **An AI-powered learning platform that transforms static study material into an interactive, real-world and adaptive learning experience.**

---

## 💡 About LearnQuest

LearnQuest transforms traditional PDFs and study notes into an engaging learning journey.

Instead of simply reading and memorizing academic content, students learn concepts through **real-world examples, visual micro-lessons, interactive challenges, and personalized feedback**.

The platform analyzes learning performance, identifies weak concepts, provides targeted re-learning, and allows students to master a topic before progressing to the next one.

### Our Goal

**Learn the concept. Don't just memorize the notes.**

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Students often depend on passive reading and memorization of PDFs and notes.

This makes it difficult to:

- Understand complex concepts deeply
- Relate academic concepts to real-world situations
- Identify weak areas
- Practice concepts interactively
- Track learning progress
- Know when a topic is actually mastered

There is a need for a more **interactive, personalized, and application-oriented learning experience**.
